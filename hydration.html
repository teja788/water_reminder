Can you drink too much water?

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Yes — though it is uncommon, and it is almost always about how fast you drink rather than how much you drink over a whole day.

This page is general information, not medical advice. If you feel unwell, speak to a doctor or pharmacist — especially if you take medication or have a kidney, liver or heart condition.

What actually goes wrong

Water dilutes the sodium in your blood. Healthy kidneys can clear roughly 0.8–1.0 litres of water an hour, so drinking faster than that leaves the excess in your bloodstream, where it thins sodium below the normal range. Doctors call this hyponatremia; when it comes from drinking, it is often called water intoxication. Cases have been reported after roughly 3–4 litres inside an hour or two.

Early signs

  • Nausea or vomiting
  • A bloated, uncomfortable stomach
  • Headache
  • Drowsiness, confusion, or muscle weakness

Left untreated it can progress to seizures and worse, so these are worth taking seriously rather than waiting out. See a healthcare provider promptly if you have them after drinking a lot of water.

Who is more at risk

  • Endurance athletes and anyone working long hours in heat
  • People taking some antidepressants (including SSRIs) or antipsychotics
  • People with kidney, liver or heart conditions, or SIADH
  • Infants under one year old

So how much should I drink?

There is no official upper limit for water. For guidance on what is enough, EFSA puts adequate total water intake at about 2.0 litres a day for women and 2.5 for men; the US Institute of Medicine puts it higher, at about 2.7 and 3.7 litres. Both figures include water from food, which is usually around a fifth of the total. Sweating hard raises the number, sometimes a lot.

For exercise specifically, the Wilderness Medical Society's guidelines recommend drinking to thirst rather than to a fixed schedule, because sweat losses vary far too much between people for a fixed number to be safe. Salty snacks alongside fluid help, but they do not prevent the problem if you are overdrinking.
