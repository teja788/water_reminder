## Description
Sip is the water reminder that respects you.

Most hydration apps nag you all day, interrupt you with ads, and ask you to create an account. Sip does none of that.

SMART REMINDERS THAT STOP
Set your wake and sleep times once. Sip spaces gentle reminders through your day — and the moment you reach your goal, they stop. Hit your goal by lunch? Silence until tomorrow.

LOG WITHOUT OPENING THE APP
Tap "Log 250 ml" right on the reminder notification. Done.

ONE-TAP TRACKING
Three preset buttons and a custom amount. An animated glass fills as your day progresses. Undo mistakes with one tap.

A GOAL THAT FITS YOU
Enter your weight and activity level for a suggested daily goal, or set your own. Works in ml or oz.

YOUR HISTORY, FOREVER
A 7-day chart, streak tracking, and a complete history of every day you've logged — stored only on your device.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No analytics. No cloud. Your hydration data stays on your iPhone, full stop.

Free, with no catches. Drink up.

## App Review notes
- No account or sign-in; all data is stored locally on device (AsyncStorage). No servers, no data collection.
- Notification permission is requested during onboarding; the app is fully usable if declined (reminders simply stay off).
- To test the headline feature: complete onboarding, background the app; a scheduled reminder notification includes a "Log 250 ml" action that logs water without opening the app.
- Uses OTA JS updates via expo-updates (Apple-permitted, JS-only).
