Water Reminder — Support

Common questions

Reminders stopped after I met my goal — is that a bug?

No — that's the point. Water Reminder stops nudging you once today's goal is met, and starts again tomorrow.

I'm not getting any reminders

Check that notifications are allowed: iOS Settings → Notifications → Water Reminder → Allow Notifications. Then open the app once so it can reschedule. If the app hasn't been opened for a couple of days, reminders pause until the next open.

Can I sync between devices or back up my history?

Not in this version. All data lives on your device only — see the privacy policy.
