Water Reminder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your device. From version 1.3, the app also sends anonymous usage counts with nothing that identifies you.

What the app stores

Your settings (weight, activity level, daily goal, cup sizes, schedule) and your drink log are saved in the app's private storage on your device only. There is no account, no sign-up, and no cloud sync.

What the app does not do

  • Your drink log and settings are never transmitted off your device.
  • No tracking, no advertising, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No third parties receive any information.

Anonymous usage counts

From version 1.3, the app tells us how it is used, as totals with nothing that identifies you, so we can see what helps and fix what doesn't.

  • What is sent: a few times a day, a short message with that day's counts: how many drinks were logged (and how many from a reminder or the widget), whether setup was finished, whether the daily goal was reached, how often a drink was undone or a reminder snoozed, and how long the app was open. Amounts, times and your goal are not sent. The first message of a day also says the app version, the kind of device (iPhone or iPad), the app's language, and how many days ago the app was installed.
  • What is never sent: your drink log, weight, goal, schedule, a device or advertising identifier, an account, your location, or anything else that could single you out. The messages carry no code that links one day's message to another or to you.
  • What we keep: our server adds each message to that day's totals for the whole app (for example "312 drinks, 41 devices") and does not keep the message itself. The network (IP) address a message comes from is used only in the server's working memory, for up to an hour, to stop floods; it is never stored or logged.
  • Offline: the counts wait on your device and go with the next message once there is a connection. Counts more than a week old are deleted unsent.
  • Hosting: Cloudflare Workers, under Cloudflare's privacy policy, with request logging turned off.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device using iOS local notifications. They do not involve any server.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data.
